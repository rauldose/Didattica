Soft of exercise programmable multimediale with the followings characteristics:

- simplicity in the programming of the exercises;
- multimedialità with the custom of lines graphic .BMP and of lines audio .WAV;
- adaptability at the learning of all the disciplines;
- make particularly stimulating and socializating the learning;
- facility of exchange of the exercises multimediali between schools (it is moved only the directory with the dates of the exercise);

STRUCTURE
The soft tramp the scheme of the videogames it in fact has formed by levels (five) surmountable to the attainment of a dots established. For each level could be inserted:
- the necessary points for the overcome of the level;
- the acquired points for each exact exercise;
- the points lost for wrong answers;
- the points lost for have not answered in the established time;
- given time for answer;
- time of gained play.
Each level is composed from:
- introduction: initial part (text, graphic or sound) the use for explain the pupil the work to execute in that level. With the introduction an external soft could also be activated.
- delivery : part (text, graphic or sound) common to a block of exercises;
- exercises.
The exercises are composed from one or more questions. The composed exercise from an only question is simplest programmable structure. It's composed by question (text, graphic or sound) and answer. The pupil could furnish the answer by four operative ways :
1) fingering ;
2) selecting ;
3) choosing from a list ;
4) clicking in a portion of video;
In the modality 1, in phase of programming, they could be inserted up to five correct solution.
When the exercise is composed from more questions (max 7), these has presented in sequence. The questions could present themselves in an only block or in more parts away away that the pupil answers. When the questions are in an only block of text it is possible insert of the lines always in the text that will come replaces by the varied solutions.
The exercises could be executed in sequential order or in casual order.
When the exercises have executed in casual order, the soft propose again those resolved in wrong way up to when not they come resolved correctly.
The resolution of the exercise has facilitated from three accessories:
- the hint;
- the launching of an external program;
- the solution;
The hint is a help (text, graphic or audio) insertable optionally in each solution and he is usable from the pupil clicking on the picture of the owl (he appears only when he is available the hint.) For do an external program, for example the prog. calc.exe (the calculator of windows) the pupil must clic on an element of the menù that appears with the presentation of the exercise.Clicking, in any moment 'solution ', they lose themselves the established points for wrong answer and appears the solution.
